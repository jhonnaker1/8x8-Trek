       U.S.S. LEXINGTON  RCB-92
         THE COMMAND CONSOLE

  Nine panels, and forty columns to
  put them on. So the console comes in
  two halves and you choose which one
  you are looking at.

  The half you fly from:

   SHORT RANGE SCAN  the sectors of
     this quadrant
   STATUS  stardate, energy, impulse,
     shields, torpedoes, warp speed,
     and Mongols left in the sector
   LASERS  efficiency and heat
   COMMAND  where you are, and the
     prompt
   MAIN VIEWER  the nearest enemy, or
     the planet you are orbiting

  Messages arrive below the command
  line, newest at the bottom.

  PRESS C FOR THE OTHER HALF: the
  chart of the known galaxy, SYSTEMS
  STATUS, and the ship's badge. Any
  key brings you back, and C costs you
  no time at all.
